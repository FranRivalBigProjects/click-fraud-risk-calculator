How to Read This

Very low conversion rate combined with a high bounce rate and very short time on site is a classic pattern of low-quality or fraudulent traffic, though it can also simply mean poor ad-to-landing-page relevance. Use this as a prompt to dig into your traffic source reports, not as definitive proof of fraud.
